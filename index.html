A cafe built around one flavour we love

Dark Chocolate Cafe started with a simple idea: coffee and chocolate deserve equal care. Every drink is prepared to order, finished with generous toppings, and served in a space that feels calm, warm, and unhurried.

Come in for a quick cup to carry, or settle into an armchair with friends and stay for the evening. Either way, we want each visit to feel like a small treat.

Chocolate first
Dark chocolate sits at the centre of our menu, from hot drinks to shakes and desserts.
Brewed with care
Coffee is prepared fresh for each order and never rushed.
Room to stay
Wide seating, soft armchairs, and tables for two or for a full group.

What we offer

From the first coffee of the day to a late dessert, everything is made to be enjoyed slowly. Stay with us or take it along.

Fresh coffee and lattes

Hot espresso drinks, cappuccinos, and lattes brewed fresh and served with care.

Chocolate shakes and cold coffee

Thick, chilled blends layered with dark chocolate drizzle and soft whipped cream.

Ice cream and frozen treats

Cones, scoops, and sundaes for anyone who needs something sweet and cold.

Pastries and desserts

Fresh pastries, cakes, and baked sweets that pair with any cup on the menu.

Dine-in seating

Comfortable armchairs, wooden chairs, and roomy tables for friends, family, and groups.

Takeaway

Securely lidded cups with paper straws, ready to carry wherever you are headed.

Warm light, hand-drawn walls, and quiet corners

The room is designed to slow things down, so a short break easily becomes the best part of your day.

Warm pendant lighting

Matte black lamps with a golden glow soften every table, day and night.

Hand-drawn coffee walls

Illustrated murals of cups, beans, and pastries give the cafe its character.

Greenery and warm wood
